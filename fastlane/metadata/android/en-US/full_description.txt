Daily wholesale (APMC mandi) crop prices from markets across India, from the government's open data on data.gov.in (Agmarknet).

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Browse by state, district and market, with min, max and modal price for every commodity the market reported</li>
<li>Search a commodity across all markets in India, in English or Hindi, and narrow it to one state</li>
<li>Favourites on the home screen with today's price and the change since the previous report</li>
<li>Price per quintal and per kg</li>
<li>30-day trend line</li>
<li>Arrival date always shown, and highlighted when a market has not reported for two days or more</li>
<li>Works offline with the last downloaded prices</li>
<li>Hindi names for common commodities</li>
<li>Material You colours and dark theme</li>
</ul>

<b>Privacy</b>
No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no account. The only network access is downloading the published price files from GitHub. Your favourites stay on your device.

Prices are updated four times a day by an open-source scheduled job that reads the data.gov.in API, so the app itself needs no API key.
