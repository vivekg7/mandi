# Privacy

Mandi has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

## Network

The app makes one kind of network request: it downloads the published price
files from **GitHub** (`raw.githubusercontent.com`, the `data` branch of
`github.com/vivekg7/mandi`). These are plain file downloads. They carry no
identifier and nothing about you, your location or your favourites. GitHub
sees the usual request metadata, such as your IP address, under
[GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

The app never contacts data.gov.in or any other server. The government data is
fetched by a GitHub Actions workflow, not by your phone.

## On your device

Your favourites and the last downloaded prices are stored only in the app's
private storage. They are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.
